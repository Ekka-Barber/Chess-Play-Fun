About move ratings

Book follows a known opening. Best matches the engine’s first choice. Excellent and Good keep most of your position’s value. Inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders lose increasing chances.

Brilliant means a sound, newly offered piece sacrifice that stays close to the best move. Great marks a critical only-good choice. These are conservative local assessments, not Chess.com’s proprietary ratings. More search can change an assessment.
